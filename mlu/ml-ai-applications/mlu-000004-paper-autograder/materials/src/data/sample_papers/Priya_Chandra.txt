My Experience in Data Structures Class

This semester has been really busy for me. I am taking four classes and also
working part time at the campus library, so finding time to study has been hard.
Data structures is my favorite class though because the professor is really nice
and explains things clearly.

I think data structures are very important for computer science. Everyone says
you need to know them to get a job at a big tech company, and the interviews ask
about them a lot. My friend who graduated last year told me that the interview
was mostly about arrays and trees and stuff like that, so I know I need to study
these topics carefully before I graduate.

The hardest part for me has been keeping up with the homework. Some weeks there
is a lot of coding and I stay up late to finish it. I have gotten better at
managing my time though. I also joined a study group which helps a lot because we
can explain things to each other.

Overall I am enjoying the class and I think I am learning a lot. I am looking
forward to the rest of the semester and to using these skills in my career. Data
structures will definitely help me become a better programmer and I am grateful
for the opportunity to learn them.
