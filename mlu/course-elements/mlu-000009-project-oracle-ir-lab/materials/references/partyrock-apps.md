# PartyRock apps used in the labs

The lab sequence uses instructor-built PartyRock apps that students interact with before building
their own. They are hosted on PartyRock rather than bundled here, since a PartyRock app is a live
hosted object rather than a file.

| App | Where it is used | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **StudySync: Exam Review Assistant** | Lab Intensive 1 — an instructor-validated LLM learning companion for exam preparation and study strategy | <https://partyrock.aws/u/greenbayinc/7Grkc2DAU/StudySync%253A-Exam-Review-Assistant> |
| **Alien Encounter! A Global Society Response Simulation** | Lab Practical 1 — the International Society scenario students analyze | <https://partyrock.aws/u/greenbayinc/ZFGjOV1gz/Alien-Encounter!%253A-A-Global-Society-Response-Simulation> |
| **PartyRock Flow Architect** | Legacy resource, retained for reference | <https://partyrock.aws/u/greenbayinc/XK8-KnliW/PartyRock-Flow-Architect> |
| **Lesson Slide to Educator Guide** | Faculty-facing utility from the author's related workshop materials | <https://partyrock.aws/u/greenbayinc/V-LHe9uiS/Lesson-Slide-to-Educator-Guide> |

Opening a shared PartyRock app requires a free sign-in but no AWS account.

> **Before you plan a delivery around these:** hosted apps can be changed or removed by their owner,
> and the models behind them change over time. Open each one and run it against your own scenario
> before you build a class period on it. The author's own evergreen note applies here — the labs
> were designed against the LLMs available in spring 2025, and the specific prompts and capabilities
> may have moved since.
