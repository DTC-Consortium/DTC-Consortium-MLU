# Changelog — mlu-000009

All notable changes to this contribution. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-19

First publication in the DTCC-MLU repository.

### Added
- `lab-project.md` — the capstone research-design project: five required sections, the Bot-Build and
  Human submission tracks with their differing AI-transparency requirements, the Cooperative
  Critique oral defense format, both 10-point rubrics, and the five defense prompts.
- Slide decks for Lab Intensive 1 (sessions 1 and 2) and Lab Practical 1.
- `pre-lecture-international-society.pdf` — the Hedley Bull three-traditions reading behind Lab
  Practical 1.
- `partyrock-apps.md` — links to the four instructor-built PartyRock apps, with a caution about
  hosted-app drift.
- `oacc-workforce-readiness-non-stem-genai.pdf` — the author's conference presentation framing the
  problem.
- `course-design-notes.md` — the author's working design notes carrying the 16-week schedule and the
  per-practical design grid.

### Known gaps
- **This is a scaffolding template, not a turnkey lab.** The author states it plainly: the module was
  designed against the LLMs publicly available in spring 2025, and specific prompts, capabilities,
  and costs may no longer hold. The structure transfers; the prompts need re-testing.
- **The design notes are incomplete by their own admission.** Several fields across Lab Practicals
  1–4 read "Not specified," and the two Lab Intensive 2 session outlines were never written.
- **Slide decks exist only for Lab Intensive 1 and Lab Practical 1.** Practicals 2–4 and Lab
  Intensive 2 are described in the design notes but have no delivered materials here.
- **PartyRock apps are linked, not bundled**, because a PartyRock app is a hosted object. They can be
  changed or removed by their owner.
