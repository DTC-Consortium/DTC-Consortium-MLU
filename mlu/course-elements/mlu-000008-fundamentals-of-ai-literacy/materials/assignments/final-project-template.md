### Sample prompt

| Element | Content |
|---|---|
| **Role** | You are an experienced workforce development specialist and career coach. |
| **Context** | I am a sophomore majoring in Criminal Justice. I am interested in careers that will remain in demand over the next ten years and that increasingly use artificial intelligence. |
| **Task** | Identify five career options. Explain: job responsibilities; required education; average salary; job outlook; skills employers seek; how AI is being used in each career. |
| **Format** | Create a comparison table followed by a summary. |
| **Constraints** | Use clear language for college students; limit your response to approximately 600 words; include examples of AI tools used in each profession. |
