# Changelog — mlu-000008

All notable changes to this contribution. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-19

First publication in the DTCC-MLU repository.

### Added
- `fundamentals-of-ai-literacy-syllabus.pdf` — the Summer 2026 syllabus, with the seven learning
  outcomes and the 30/30/30/10 module weighting.
- `final-project-template.md` / `.docx` — Module 4, the five-part prompt-engineering project:
  career pathway, one structured prompt, three tools, comparison chart, five reflection questions.
- `getting-started-with-partyrock-chatgpt-and-copilot.md` / `.docx` — illustrated step-by-step
  walkthrough for all three tools, with screenshots.
- `materials/lectures/README.md` — index of the three externally hosted video modules, and an
  explicit statement of what this contribution's licence does and does not cover.
- `materials/rubrics/final-project-rubric.md` — a 50-point rubric for the Module 4 project.
  **Not the author's work**; see Known gaps.

### Known gaps
- **The author's Module 4 rubric is missing, and the rubric shipped here is a consortium
  substitute.** Hers lives in Talladega's Canvas portal and the file failed to upload with the
  original submission. The substitute keeps her 50-point total, her five parts, and the syllabus
  outcomes; the point split across parts and all scoring guidance are the consortium's, and the
  project template now points at it instead of at a Canvas rubric no adopter can reach. Replace it
  if the author's original is released.
- **A fourth video link** from the original submission has an unconfirmed role in the course
  sequence and is recorded in the lectures index rather than listed as a module.

### Notes
- Contributed through the AWS-MLU Faculty Fellows programme and delivered at Talladega College,
  where it supports a required freshman seminar as part of embedding AI literacy across the liberal
  arts curriculum.
- The Markdown files are faithful transcriptions of the author's Word documents, reformatted for the
  repository's contribution layout. Content is unchanged and the originals ship alongside; the
  embedded screenshots were extracted and given descriptive filenames.
- The video modules incorporate AWS/MLU material used with authorization and are linked rather than
  redistributed. They are excluded from the `CC-BY-4.0` grant that covers the rest of this
  contribution.
