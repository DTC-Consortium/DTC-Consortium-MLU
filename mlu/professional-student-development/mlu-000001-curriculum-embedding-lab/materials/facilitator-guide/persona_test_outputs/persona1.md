# Teaching Case Vignettes for 3rd-Year Dental Students

## Vignette 1: Foundational

### Case Presentation
A 25-year-old female patient presents with severe gum recession and swelling around tooth 11. She reports progressive discomfort and difficulty maintaining oral hygiene. Clinical examination reveals gingival recession and a probing depth of 10 mm on the mesial aspect of tooth 11.

### Discussion Questions
1. What is the primary finding in this case, and what is the most obvious treatment decision?
2. How would you explain the cause of the gingival recession and swelling to the patient?

## Vignette 2: Typical

### Case Presentation
A 33-year-old male patient presents with progressively increasing gingival recession and swelling affecting tooth 11. He has a history of fixed orthodontic treatment that lasted three years. Clinical examination reveals severe gingival recession, probing depths greater than 5.5 mm, and tooth mobility. A dental panoramic tomography (DPT) shows generalized moderate bone loss.

### Differential Diagnoses
1. Localized periodontitis
2. Periodontal abscess
3. Primary periodontal-secondary endodontic lesion

### Discussion Questions
1. Based on the clinical findings, which of the differential diagnoses is most likely?
2. What are the key factors that support the diagnosis of Stage IV, Grade C periodontitis?
3. What are the potential outcomes and treatment options for this patient?

## Vignette 3: Complex

### Case Presentation
A 33-year-old male ex-smoker with a history of asthma presents with severe gingival recession and swelling around tooth 11. He has a history of fixed orthodontic treatment and irregular dental follow-up visits. Clinical examination reveals severe gingival recession, probing depths greater than 5.5 mm, and tooth mobility. A dental panoramic tomography (DPT) shows generalized moderate bone loss.

### Discussion Questions
1. Considering the patient's history and clinical findings, what are the potential challenges in managing this case?
2. What are the multiple defensible treatment paths for this patient, and what factors would influence your decision?
3. How would you address the patient's concerns about tooth loss and the impact on his quality of life?
4. Given the patient's history of smoking and current asthma, what ethical considerations should be taken into account when planning the treatment?
