# Prof. Diane Okafor — Nursing Med-Calc Drill

### Medication Calculation Practice Problems

#### 1. Digoxin (Cardiac Glycoside)
**Patient Details:**  
65-year-old male with congestive heart failure

**Drug Name:**  
Digoxin

**Dosing Parameters:**  
Usual adult dosing: 0.125 mg to 0.25 mg daily

**Clinical Scenario Setup:**  
The patient has been prescribed digoxin 0.125 mg PO daily for heart failure management.

**Calculation Question:**  
Order: Digoxin 0.125 mg PO daily.  
Available: 0.25 mg scored tablets.

**Answer with Worked Solution:**  
Calculation: 0.125 mg ÷ 0.25 mg per tablet = 0.5 tablet (one-half of a scored tablet)

**⚠️ SAFETY RED FLAG:**  
Hold the dose and notify the prescriber if the apical heart rate is below 60 beats per minute.

#### 2. Furosemide (Loop Diuretic)
**Patient Details:**  
50-year-old female with acute pulmonary edema

**Drug Name:**  
Furosemide

**Dosing Parameters:**  
Usual adult dosing: 20 mg to 80 mg per dose

**Clinical Scenario Setup:**  
The patient needs furosemide 40 mg IV push for acute pulmonary edema.

**Calculation Question:**  
Order: Furosemide 40 mg IV now.  
Available: Furosemide 10 mg/mL.

**Answer with Worked Solution:**  
Calculation: 40 mg ÷ 10 mg per mL = 4 mL

**⚠️ SAFETY RED FLAG:**  
Administer no faster than 20 mg per minute to reduce the risk of ototoxicity.

#### 3. Heparin (Anticoagulant)
**Patient Details:**  
70-year-old male with deep vein thrombosis

**Drug Name:**  
Heparin

**Dosing Parameters:**  
Usual adult dosing: 80 units/kg for a bolus, 18 units/kg/hr for continuous infusion

**Clinical Scenario Setup:**  
The patient needs a heparin bolus for deep vein thrombosis.

**Calculation Question:**  
Order: Heparin bolus 80 units/kg for a patient weighing 70 kg.  
Available: Heparin 1,000 units/mL.

**Answer with Worked Solution:**  
Calculation: 80 units/kg × 70 kg = 5,600 units  
Volume = 5,600 units ÷ 1,000 units per mL = 5.6 mL

**⚠️ SAFETY RED FLAG:**  
Ensure the aPTT is within the therapeutic range before administering the bolus.

#### 4. Heparin (Continuous Infusion)
**Patient Details:**  
68-year-old female with atrial fibrillation

**Drug Name:**  
Heparin

**Dosing Parameters:**  
Usual adult dosing: 18 units/kg/hr

**Clinical Scenario Setup:**  
The patient needs a heparin infusion for atrial fibrillation.

**Calculation Question:**  
Order: Heparin infusion 18 units/kg/hr for a patient weighing 70 kg.  
Available: 25,000 units in 250 mL (100 units/mL).

**Answer with Worked Solution:**  
Calculation: 18 units/kg/hr × 70 kg = 1,260 units/hr  
Rate = 1,260 units/hr ÷ 100 units per mL = 12.6 mL/hr

**⚠️ SAFETY RED FLAG:**  
Monitor aPTT levels regularly and adjust the infusion rate as necessary.

#### 5. Aspirin (Antiplatelet)
**Patient Details:**  
55-year-old male with suspected myocardial infarction

**Drug Name:**  
Aspirin

**Dosing Parameters:**  
Usual adult dosing: 324 mg loading dose

**Clinical Scenario Setup:**  
The patient needs an aspirin loading dose for a suspected myocardial infarction.

**Calculation Question:**  
Order: Chewable aspirin 324 mg now.  
Available: 81 mg chewable tablets.

**Answer with Worked Solution:**  
Calculation: 324 mg ÷ 81 mg per tablet = 4 tablets

**⚠️ SAFETY RED FLAG:**  
Avoid in patients with active bleeding or aspirin hypersensitivity.
