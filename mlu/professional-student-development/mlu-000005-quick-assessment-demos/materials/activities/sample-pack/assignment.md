# Assignment — Choose the Right Structure

> *Synthetic sample data — no real individuals or institutions. For training use only.*

**Course:** Introduction to Data Structures (sophomore level)
**Reading:** *Open Data Structures*, §1.5 (running times) and §2.1 (ArrayStack) —
`persona2_cs_data_structures.pdf`
**Length:** one to two pages (about 200–800 words)
**Due:** end of Week 4

## The prompt

Pick one operation that a real program performs over and over — for example, checking whether an
item has been seen before, adding to the end of a list, inserting into the middle of a list, or
looking up the *i*-th item. Argue which data structure is the right choice for a program dominated
by that operation, and why.

Your paper must:

1. **State a thesis** that names the operation and the structure you recommend for it.
2. **Give the running time** of that operation for your structure and for at least one
   alternative, in big-O notation, and say which kind of guarantee each one is — worst-case,
   amortized, or expected (§1.5).
3. **Ground the argument in one concrete scenario** — a real program or task.
4. **Name at least one situation where your recommended structure is the worse choice.**
5. **Refer to the course reading** at least once where it supports a claim.

## Learning objectives

- **LO1** — Select a data structure for a program's dominant operation and justify the choice
  against an alternative.
- **LO2** — State running times in asymptotic notation and distinguish worst-case, amortized, and
  expected guarantees.
- **LO3** — Support technical claims with a concrete scenario and evidence from the course reading.
- **LO4** — Communicate a technical argument clearly to a peer.
